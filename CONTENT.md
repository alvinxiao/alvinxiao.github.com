# Content specification and editorial controls

## Voice
Friendly, practical, collaborative, personal, concise, non-salesy. Explain technical topics in plain English. Professional about scope and deliverables. Use first person singular ('I'), not 'we'. Avoid em dashes, buzzwords, inflated claims, invented outcomes, false urgency, and generic AI hype.

## Approved homepage hero
Eyebrow: **Practical AI help for small businesses**

Headline: **Let's figure out how AI can help your business.**

Supporting copy: **You don't need to be a tech expert to get started. I help small business owners understand AI, work through everyday challenges, and find practical solutions that make their work easier.**

Primary CTA: **Tell me what you're working on**
Secondary CTA: **Explore services**

## Approved two paths
**I'm new to AI**
Curious about AI but not sure where to begin? I'll help you understand the tools, explore examples relevant to your work, and get comfortable trying them yourself.
CTA: **Explore AI Getting Started**

**I have a problem to solve**
Spending too much time on repetitive work or wondering whether a process could be easier? Let's look at what's happening and explore practical ways to improve it.
CTA: **Explore Problem-Solving**

## Homepage service summaries
- **AI Getting Started:** Learn the basics of AI through practical examples and a personalized, hands-on session.
- **AI Problem-Solving:** Bring a specific challenge and work through possible tools, approaches, and next steps.
- **AI Workflow Audit:** Take a closer look at one business process to identify opportunities for improvement.
- **Custom Automation:** Get help implementing a solution when the right next step involves connecting tools or automating tasks.

## Illustrative examples (not client case studies)
- Repeated customer questions: AI-assisted drafting, FAQ, or reusable email templates may be appropriate.
- Copying data between spreadsheets: formulas, scripts, or basic automation may work without AI.
- Organizing documents: AI-assisted summaries, templates, or a better process may help.
Clearly label all as illustrative. Do not imply results or clients.

## Working together
1. **Tell me what you're working on.** Share what you want to learn or what isn't working well.
2. **We'll explore your options.** I'll help you understand what tools or approaches make sense.
3. **Leave with practical next steps.** Depending on the service, you'll receive guidance, recommendations, or a written assessment.

## Personal introduction: blocked pending owner copy
Display **no fabricated personal biography**. Development-only placeholders:
- `PUBLIC_NAME_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`
- `PROFESSIONAL_DESCRIPTOR_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` (working descriptor: AI & Automation Consulting)
- `BIO_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`
- `CONTACT_EMAIL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`
- `NOTIFICATION_EMAIL_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`
No production deployment with these tokens, generic headshots, or invented qualifications. If the biography is still unavailable, remove the About/personal intro content from launch scope only with explicit owner approval and update navigation accordingly.

## Resource plan
Target starter set: one AI fundamentals guide, one practical getting-started guide, one workflow walkthrough comparing AI and non-AI options. Resources are optional for initial core-page launch but must not link to nonexistent articles. Keep article content in Markdown; require factual review for new AI tool claims and time-sensitive details.

## Content governance
Keep business facts in typed central configuration, articles in Markdown, layout copy in controlled content files where practical. Agent edits must produce a PR with content diff. Changes to offers, prices, biography, factual background, privacy terms, and guarantees require explicit owner approval. Run production checks for placeholder tokens and unapproved flags.
