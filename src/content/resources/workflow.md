---
title: 'A better workflow may not need AI'
description: 'An illustrative comparison of templates, simple automation, and AI-assisted drafting.'
category: 'Think it through'
---

This is an illustrative scenario, not a client case study or a claim about results.

Imagine you answer the same delivery questions every week. Before choosing a tool, look at why the questions repeat and what each answer needs.

## Option one: make the answer easier to find

A clear FAQ or reusable email template may be enough when the answer rarely changes. Check that the information is accurate and that someone owns keeping it current.

## Option two: automate a predictable step

If the answer comes from structured information, a rule-based notification or a supported integration may be appropriate. First check permissions, error handling, and how someone can correct a wrong message.

## Option three: use AI for a reviewed draft

When questions vary and a person needs to judge the answer, AI-assisted drafting might be worth exploring. Keep sensitive information out of the experiment and review every draft before sending it.

## Choose by the problem

Compare the effort, reliability, privacy implications, and maintenance of each option. It is reasonable to improve the process without adding a new tool.
