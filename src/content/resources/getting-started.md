---
title: 'Choose one useful place to start'
description: 'A practical way to explore AI without changing your whole business at once.'
category: 'Try something small'
---

You do not need an AI strategy before you can ask a useful question. Begin with one repeated task and decide what a better result would look like.

## Write down the task

Describe what you do, what information you use, and what the result needs to be. Notice the parts that require your judgment. A tool may help with a draft while you keep responsibility for the decision.

## Choose a safe example

Use fictional or public information for your first attempt. Do not copy customer details, private documents, credentials, or payment information into a tool to see what happens.

## Compare the result with your usual approach

Check accuracy, the effort needed to revise the output, and whether the result fits your work. An impressive-looking answer can still take longer to fix than doing the task another way.

## Keep what helps

If the attempt is useful, document the instructions and review steps. If it is not, try a smaller task, a template, or a non-AI tool. An experiment that tells you what does not help is useful too.
